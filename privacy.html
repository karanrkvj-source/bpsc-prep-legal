Privacy Policy

Effective date: 13 June 2026 · Last updated: 13 June 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile application BPSC Prep: GS in 30 Days ("the App", "we", "our") handles your information. By installing or using the App, you agree to the practices described below.

Short version: We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data. Everything you do inside the App — quiz progress, scores, notes, settings — stays on your device. The only external service the App interacts with is Google Play Billing, when you choose to purchase the premium upgrade.

1. Information we do not collect

The App is offline-first and account-free. We do not collect or transmit any of the following:

  • Your name, email address, phone number, or date of birth
  • Your location (GPS, IP-based, or otherwise)
  • Contacts, photos, calendar, microphone, or camera data
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Browsing history or behavior outside the App
  • Any biometric or sensitive personal information

The App does not require you to create an account or sign in. You can use it entirely without identifying yourself.

2. Information stored locally on your device

To make the App work, the following information is stored on your device only and is never transmitted to us or to any third party:

  • Your quiz attempts, scores, and accuracy by subject
  • Your 30-day study plan progress and streak data
  • Notes, bookmarks, and saved items you create inside the App
  • App preferences such as language toggle, theme, and notification time
  • Your premium purchase status (so the App can unlock the features you paid for)

This data lives in a local database on your device. Uninstalling the App permanently deletes all of it. We cannot recover it for you, and we cannot transfer it to a new device.

3. Permissions the App requests

Notifications

If you enable the daily study reminder, the App uses Android's notification system to alert you at a time you choose. Notifications are scheduled and shown by your device — they do not contact any server.

Billing

If you purchase the premium upgrade, the App uses Google Play Billing to process the transaction. We never see or store your payment method, card number, or billing address. Google's privacy practices govern that transaction — please refer to Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Third-party services

The App uses exactly one third-party service:

  • Google Play Billing — for processing in-app purchases. Required by Google Play Store policy for paid features.

The App does not use Firebase Analytics, Google Analytics, advertising networks, crash reporting services, or any other third-party analytics or tracking SDK.

5. Children's privacy

The App is intended for users aged 18 and above who are preparing for the Bihar Public Service Commission examination. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13 years of age. Because the App collects no personal information from anyone, this policy applies equally regardless of the user's age.

6. Your rights and choices

  • Right to delete: Uninstall the App to permanently delete all data stored on your device.
  • Right to know: All data the App holds about you is visible inside the App itself — Subjects, Analytics, Notes, Settings.
  • Right to opt out: You can disable notifications, restore purchases, or stop using the App at any time.

Because we do not collect or transmit your data, there is no off-device record for us to delete, export, or correct.

7. Security

Your data is stored in your device's private app storage, which is sandboxed by the Android operating system from other apps. We strongly recommend keeping your device's operating system updated and using a screen lock to protect your data.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be communicated through an in-App notice in the Settings → About section before they take effect. Continued use of the App after a change indicates acceptance of the updated policy.
