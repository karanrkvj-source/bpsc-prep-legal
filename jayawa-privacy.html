JayaWa Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 October 2026

JayaWa is an offline running game. The game does not collect, transmit, sell or share personal data.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your best score, sound and graphics preferences, vibration preference, and a checkpoint for an unfinished run in local application storage. This information stays on your device. It is not sent to the developer or a third party.

Permissions and third-party services

JayaWa uses vibration permission for optional gameplay feedback. Vibration can be disabled in the game's settings. The app does not request Internet permission. It has no advertising, analytics, account registration, in-app purchases, or third-party tracking SDKs. Its bundled artwork and 3D character load from the app itself.

Deleting saved information

You can delete the game's locally stored information using Android Settings → Apps → JayaWa → Storage → Clear data, or by uninstalling the app. Clearing data also removes your local score and saved run.

Children's information

The game does not collect personal information from players of any age.

Changes
